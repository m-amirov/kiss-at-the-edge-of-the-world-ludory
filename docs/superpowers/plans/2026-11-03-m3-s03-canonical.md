## Design and tradeoffs

- Retain original literary text byte-for-byte: 10 blocks, two conditional reaction groups, one authored choice with two distinct outcomes. No invented dialogue or romantic route lock.
- **N1 limitation:** `@ludory/narrative` 1.2.2 supports `when` only for choice variants/echo, not unconditional dialogue beats or automatic conditional transitions. We cannot switch conditional S03 text on a single shared graph path natively.
- **Chosen bounded game-only adaptation:** at each conditional point render one and only one `Продолжить` option, with mutually exclusive flag predicates. This is a deterministic technical navigation gate, *not* a new authored player choice or new story effect. It requires an extra click but preserves source variants and state-log provenance. Regression tests must prove exactly one available option for all 9 S01×S02 combinations.
- Alternative rejected for this slice: duplicating S01→S65 graph 9 times (huge duplication), or changing Ludory N1/runtime/template contracts now (requires separate framework PR + update trust flow). The gate is explicitly temporary and subject to product UX review later.
- Source S03-C1 A/B has no specified relationship score or route intent effects. Use exclusive persistent `s03-damir-message` / `s03-damir-silence` tracking flags only, with no romance/affinity deltas.
- The S65 technical ending is removed to transition to S03. New technical ending goes after the two authored S03-C1 variants, before not-yet-migrated S04. Previous prototype save terminal checkpoints may be incompatible; do not silently rewrite or import.
- English locale continues RU fallback only; no new assets, platform moderation, release, original-repository mutation or `main` merge.
